# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give and receive feedback in good faith.
- Focus on what is best for the community and project users.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language or imagery, personal attacks, sustained disruption,
publication of another person's private information, or other conduct that would reasonably be
considered inappropriate in a professional setting is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through GitHub. Maintainers may
remove, edit, reject, or revert contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior violates this policy. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
reasonably possible.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
